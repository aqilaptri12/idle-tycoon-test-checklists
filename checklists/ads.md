# Checklist: Ads (Reward & Interstitial)

## ✅ Core Checks
- [ ] Reward ad appears when tapping the button
- [ ] After watching → reward is granted
- [ ] Interstitial appears at appropriate moments
- [ ] Close button works
- [ ] Ad cooldown works

## 🐛 Edge Cases
- [ ] Ad fails to load → button is disabled
- [ ] Closing ad early → reward not granted (correct)
- [ ] Watching 5 ads in a row → no crash
